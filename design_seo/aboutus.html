Our journey of innovation and growth.

From our founding to becoming the world's largest independent MSSP, our history is defined by milestones that shaped the cybersecurity industry.

1995

Trustwave Founded

Trustwave was established with a vision to provide comprehensive security solutions to businesses facing emerging digital threats.

2004

SpiderLabs Established

Launched our elite security research team, SpiderLabs, dedicated to threat intelligence, vulnerability research, and incident response.

2011

Global Expansion Milestone

Expanded operations to serve clients in over 50 countries, establishing regional security operations centers worldwide.

2015

MDR Services Launch

Introduced Managed Detection and Response (MDR) services, revolutionizing how organizations approach threat detection and incident response.

2020

Industry Recognition

Named a Leader by major analyst firms including Gartner, Forrester, and IDC for our comprehensive security platform and services.

2024

LevelBlue Acquisition

Acquired by LevelBlue to form the world's largest pure-play managed security services provider, serving over 5,000 organizations globally.
