FAQs

What is a Managed Detection and Response service?
Managed Detection and Response (MDR) is a cybersecurity service that combines technology and human expertise to perform threat hunting, monitoring, and response. The main benefit of MDR is that it helps rapidly identify and limit the impact of threats without the need for additional staffing.
What are the benefits of an MDR service?
MDR services provide 24/7/365 threat detection and response, access to cybersecurity experts, advanced threat intelligence, faster incident response times, and reduced operational costs compared to building an in-house security operations center. It also helps organizations stay ahead of evolving threats with proactive threat hunting.
I've got lots of security tools. Why do I need an MDR service?
Security tools alone cannot protect your organization. They generate alerts that require expert analysis and response. MDR services provide the skilled analysts and proven processes to maximize your existing security investments, identifying threats that automated tools miss and responding before damage occurs.
I'm using multiple cloud-based services and still have significant on-premises infrastructure. Can a single Managed Detection and Response service cover all of that?
Yes. Trustwave MDR is designed to protect hybrid environments, providing unified visibility and threat detection across cloud, on-premises, and hybrid infrastructures. Our platform integrates with all major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and on-premises technologies to deliver comprehensive protection.
I'm already spending a lot of money on cyber security. How can I cost-justify a Managed Detection and Response service on top of everything else?
MDR actually maximizes your existing security investments by ensuring tools are properly configured and monitored. The cost of a breach far exceeds MDR expenses - the average cost of a data breach is $4.45 million. MDR prevents breaches, reduces dwell time, and provides expertise without the overhead of hiring and training security staff.
What are the key attributes to look for in a Managed Detection and Response provider? What questions should I ask prospective providers?
Look for proven threat intelligence capabilities, 24/7/365 coverage, experience with your technology stack, transparent SLAs, rapid onboarding processes, and analyst expertise. Ask about their threat hunting methodology, mean time to detect/respond, technology integrations, and how they handle incident escalation and communication.
How long will it take me to get started with a Managed Detection and Response provider?
With Trustwave, onboarding typically takes days, not weeks or months. Our proven methodology includes rapid deployment processes that get you protected quickly while minimizing disruption to your operations. The exact timeline depends on your environment's complexity and existing security infrastructure.
